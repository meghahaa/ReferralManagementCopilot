CLINICAL POLICY DIRECTIVE: CARDIOLOGY REFERRALS & PRIOR AUTHORIZATION (POLICY-CARD-2026)

1. OVERVIEW
This policy outlines medical necessity and network referral criteria for adult Cardiology consults within Apex Health Network.

2. ROUTINE CARDIOLOGY CONSULTATIONS
- Indication: Essential hypertension resistant to 2+ anti-hypertensive medications, dyslipidemia with high ASCVD risk, mild stable angina.
- Requirements: Completed primary care intake, baseline ECG within 60 days, serum creatinine, and lipid panel.
- Prior Authorization: NOT required for routine in-network consults.

3. ADVANCED CARDIAC DIAGNOSTICS & MRI
- Indication: Suspected cardiomyopathy, myocardial viability assessment, complex congenital heart disease.
- Requirements: Prior Echocardiogram report, recent Cardiology specialist recommendation.
- Prior Authorization: MANDATORY PRIOR AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED. Submission of CPT code 75561 requires documentation of non-diagnostic echocardiogram.

4. URGENT / EXPEDITED REFERRALS
- Criteria: Unstable angina, acute onset congestive heart failure symptoms, troponin elevation without ST elevation.
- SLA: Referral copilot must triage and schedule within 24 hours. Emergency cases (STEMI, acute collapse) must be directed immediately to the Emergency Department (911).

5. OUT-OF-NETWORK CARDIOLOGY EXCEPTIONS
- Out-of-network consults require prior authorization approval unless patient is experiencing acute chest pain during travel outside covered zip codes.
